# Server Rules

These are the current rules shown by the live `/rules` menu, with brief player
guidance. The in-game rules and staff instructions take precedence if this page
has not yet caught up with a change.

1. **No griefing.** Do not damage, steal from, or interfere with another player's
   build or claim without permission.
2. **No mods, hacks, macros, or automated scripts.** Do not automate play or gain
   an unfair advantage. Ask staff before using a mod when unsure.
3. **Respect other players.** Harassment, threats, impersonation, and deliberately
   targeting another player are not acceptable.
4. **No spamming.** Do not flood chat, commands, messages, or server systems.
5. **No advertising.** Do not promote other servers, services, or communities
   without staff approval.
6. **No cursing or constant cursing.** Keep chat suitable for the community and
   follow staff direction.
7. **No racist or sexist remarks.** Hate speech and discriminatory remarks are not
   allowed.
8. **Do not ask for OP, ranks, or items.** Use approved progression, purchase, or
   application channels.
9. **Obey staff.** Follow instructions during moderation and investigations. Raise
   disagreements calmly through an appropriate support channel.

## Claims, trading, and exploits

- Claim important builds and containers with Residence.
- Grant claim access only to trusted players and review it with `/res info`.
- Trade only legitimate items and describe trades honestly.
- Do not exploit bugs, duplication, economy mistakes, alternate accounts, or
  permission errors. Report serious exploits privately with `/helpop <message>`.
- Do not evade a mute, ban, cooldown, claim restriction, or staff action.

## Staff and builders

The live rules require builders and staff to use privileges responsibly, never
create items for players, trade only legitimate items, use legitimate materials
when helping with builds, and never abuse power.

## Reporting a problem

Include the player name, approximate time, location, and relevant evidence. Do
not retaliate while waiting for staff assistance.
