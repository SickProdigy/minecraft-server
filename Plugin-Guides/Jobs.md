# Jobs Reborn

**Purpose:** Pays players money, points, and experience for configured activities.

**Loaded version:** See the current [Active Plugin Directory](../Plugins.md).

## McEcon access

Regular users have `jobs.use` and configured auto-join/world nodes. Start with `/jobs` or `/jobs help`; use its menus to browse, join, leave, inspect stats, quests, and rankings. VIP, builder, and staff inherit player access. `jobs.admin` and `jobs.*` belong to admin.
